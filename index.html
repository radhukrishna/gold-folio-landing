Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Your gold records stay yours

Jewellery inventories are deeply personal. Gold Folio is architected so your weights, ornament photos, and locker locations remain readable only by you.

Full Data Sovereignty

Because your ledger backups live inside your personal Zoho WorkDrive folder—and primary encryption runs on your local device—you retain full ownership and portability of your records at all times.

01

Local Device AES-GCM Encryption

Ledger data on your local device is protected using authenticated AES-GCM encryption, while copies synced to your Zoho WorkDrive are stored in your personal drive without client-side encryption.

02

No Central Ledger Database

Gold Folio does not harvest or store your jewellery inventory on central servers. Your primary encrypted vault resides locally on your device, with backup copies synced directly to your own Zoho WorkDrive.

03

Keep Your Holdings Private in Public

Comparing prices in public? Your portfolio values remain hidden by default—reveal them with a single tap only when you need them, with automatic re-locking the moment you leave the app.

Legal Disclosure

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Applies to gold-folio.co.in and app.gold-folio.co.in.

1. Local-First & Local Device Encryption

Gold Folio is built on a local-first model. Ledger data on your local device is encrypted in your browser using AES-GCM encryption and stored locally in your browser's internal storage.

2. User-Owned Zoho WorkDrive Sync

If you choose to enable cloud backup and multi-device sync, Gold Folio connects directly to your personal Zoho WorkDrive account using your authorization. Copies synced to your Zoho WorkDrive are stored inside your own Zoho WorkDrive account without client-side encryption. Gold Folio does not maintain a central database of your jewellery holdings or personal financial records.

3. Passkeys & Biometric Authentication

Optional biometric lock uses standard WebAuthn passkeys handled natively by your device's operating system and secure enclave. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to Gold Folio.

4. Indicative Gold Rates & Analytics

To display live market benchmarks, the app may request public gold rate feeds without transmitting your personal ledger contents. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or portfolio information.
